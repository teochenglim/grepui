# RELEASE v0.7.0: Rollups and extended retention

**Status:** planned
**Design choices (ARCH 06):** D5
**Exit gate:** 7-day window answered from the Flow sink; TTL changes are previewed, applied and audited-ready
**Depends on:** 0.6
**Requirements:** R-F4 R-P1 R-P2 R-P3 R-X1

## Scope

- Planner choosing raw or rollup with `meta.source`
- Rollup management from vetted templates (traces RED, metrics downsampling) with backfill
- Retention view and edit per stream; tiering policy

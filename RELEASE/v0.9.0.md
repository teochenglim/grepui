# RELEASE v0.9.0: Alerts and incidents

**Status:** planned
**Design choices (ARCH 06):** D2
**Exit gate:** an alert fires once on seeded data, notifies a webhook, dedups, and a replica restart does not double-fire
**Depends on:** 0.8
**Requirements:** R-A1 R-A2 R-A3 R-A4 R-A5 R-A6 R-A7 R-A8

## Scope

- Rule engine: builder/SQL/PromQL, interval or cron, look-back, cooldown
- Real-time (short interval) and composite alerts; anomaly alerts
- Destinations, templates, test-send; dedup, compare-with-past, downtimes, history
- Incidents; leader lease for HA

## Notes

Real-time here means short-interval polling of GreptimeDB (default 10 s), not an ingest hook; ARCH 07 states the difference.
